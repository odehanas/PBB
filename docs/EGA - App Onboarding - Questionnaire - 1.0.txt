Application Onboarding Questionnaire
Data and Insight Department  ·  Electronic Government Authority
Purpose and Scope

Generative AI coding tools have made it substantially easier for government departments to develop their own applications. The Electronic Government Authority (EGA) welcomes this. It also means that applications now reach EGA for hosting without necessarily having passed through the design, review and testing stages that a procured system would ordinarily undergo.
This questionnaire exists so that EGA can establish, consistently and without presumption, what it needs to know before accepting such an application onto government infrastructure. It applies to any application developed in-house by a department or entity, including one developed wholly or partly with the assistance of generative AI coding tools. It does not replace the procurement and assurance process applicable to systems supplied by an external vendor under contract.
The information provided will form the basis of EGA's technical and security assessment and will be discussed at a subsequent review meeting with the submitting department. Please complete all applicable items. Where a question does not apply, please indicate this rather than leaving it blank. Incomplete answers will delay the review rather than expedite it.
