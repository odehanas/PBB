1. Logging In & Choosing Your Context

  1. Sign in with the username and password from your administrator.
  2. Select your Context: Budget Year, Entity, Department. You only see your assigned scope.
  3. This selection stays active for your session and drives every screen.
Figure 1 — Sign in with the credentials from your administrator.
Figure 2 — Budget Setup: choose the Budget Year, then your Cost Center to start entry.

2. Understanding Programs & Activities

A program is a block of work in your entity; an activity is specific work inside a program. You always pick a program first, then an activity that belongs to it.

  • Mandate programs are your core mission work.
  • Support programs are shared/overhead work; you budget them the same way.
What you seeThe program list shows only the active programs of your own entity. If one is missing, ask your administrator to add or activate it.

3. Entering a Budget

  1. Open Budget Entry and pick the category: Revenue, CAPEX, OPEX or HR.
  2. Select the Program, then the Activity (the list filters to that program).
  3. Choose the line Item (account) and enter amounts.
  4. Spread the amount across months — equally or per month.
  5. For CAPEX, attach a supporting document (up to 5 MB) and set depreciation details.
  6. Save. The line appears in the recent-lines list for the current context.
Figure 3 — Budget Entry: pick the category tab, then Item, Activity, amounts and monthly distribution.

4. Submitting for Approval

  1. When lines are complete, submit the budget for your context.
  2. After submission the budget is locked and cannot be edited.
  3. If changes are needed, ask your administrator to return it; then edit and resubmit.
Figure 4 — Use the Submit for Approval button (top right) once your lines are complete.

5. Reports Available to You

You can view reports for your own scope (such as income statement and budget summaries). Cross-entity tools — Cost Allocation and the Management Review pack — are for administrators only.

Figure 5 — Executive Summary: totals by entity, expense mix and the Revenue → Net Position waterfall.

6. FAQ & Troubleshooting

I can't find a program in the dropdown.

It may be inactive or belong to a different entity. Contact your administrator.

The Save button is disabled / I see a "locked" message.

The budget has been submitted. Ask your administrator to return it for editing.

I don't see the admin menus.

Those screens are limited to administrators. Your role is USER.
